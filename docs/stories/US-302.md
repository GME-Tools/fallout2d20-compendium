Status: not started. Dependencies: US-000, US-102, US-105.

Align the 60-page English adventure and 31 scanned French double pages.
Inventory Actors, Items, attacks, abilities, inventories, and random tables;
compare each occurrence with errata-corrected Core and classify reprints,
variants, corrections, translation differences, and ambiguity. Exclude

Core reprints reuse canonical identities; mechanical variants require reviewed
new identities and names. Owner approval is required before US-304.
