| 2026-09-07 | French weights and carrying capacity | US-103 owner validation | Apply the French Core convention exactly as `kg = lb / 2` to Item weights, robot carry modifiers and Actor capacity. Preserve fractional modifiers and 25 kg encumbrance increments. | Accepted |
